# Convert Units

Converts the measurements you select — kilometres to miles, kilos to pounds, Celsius to
Fahrenheit.

## What it does

Select a sentence, a paragraph, or a clipboard card, and choose **Convert units**. Every
measurement it finds is rewritten in place, in whichever direction leads away from what
was written:

- **Length** km ↔ mi, m ↔ ft, yd → m, cm/mm → in, in → cm
- **Weight** kg ↔ lb, g ↔ oz
- **Temperature** °C ↔ °F

Both short forms (`km`, `lb`) and spelled-out names (`kilometers`, `pounds`) are
recognised. Results are rounded to two decimals and keep the spacing you wrote; a unit
spelled out becomes its short form. `5 in the morning` is left alone — `in` only converts
where a phrase ends. A number written with thousands separators is kept whole, and its
separators come along.

If nothing in the selection converts, a short message says so.

## What it touches

Only the text you selected. No folder, no network, no settings.
