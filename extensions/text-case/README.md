# Text Case

Fixes the capitalization of what you select — upper, lower, title, or sentence case.

## What it does

Four buttons appear beside a selection that contains letters:

- **Uppercase** and **Lowercase** do what they say.
- **Title Case** capitalizes the words that carry meaning and leaves the small ones — *the*,
  *of*, *and* — lowercase unless they open or close the title.
- **Sentence case** lowercases everything, then puts the first letter of each sentence back.

A word that already carries a capital of its own — iPhone, McDonald’s — keeps its spelling
instead of being treated as a word to fix. Results are written back over the selection; on a
clipboard card they are copied instead.

## What it touches

Only the text you selected. No folder, no network, no settings.
